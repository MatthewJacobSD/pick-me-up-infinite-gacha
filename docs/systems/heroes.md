---

## Hero Progression

### Stats
- **Strength**: Physical damage
- **Intelligence**: Skill learning, magic
- **Health**: Survivability
- **Agility**: Speed, evasion

### Skills
- Learn through combat and training
- Level up with use (Lv.1 → Lv.5+)
- Some heroes plateau at certain levels
- **Combat Desire**: Heroes perform better when they want to fight

### Class Evolution
- Novice → Specialized class (based on skills/stats)
- Class determines role in party

---

## Role Assignment

| Role | Function | Survival Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Combat** | Fight monsters, clear floors | Lower |
| **Support** | Cook, carpenter, craft | Higher |

### Assignment Rules
1. Check real-world skills (cooking, carpentry)
2. Assign based on utility
3. Combat roles go to strongest/most willing
4. Support roles go to skilled non-combatants

---

## Synthesis System

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Merge heroes to strengthen others |
| Cost | Sacrifice one hero |
| Benefit | Gains skill/stat boost |
| Risk | Permanent death for sacrificed hero |

### Synthesis Examples
- **Toby** → Han gains Composure
- **Yelson** → Jenna gains Eagle's Eye
- **Shay (4★)** → Han gains enhanced stats

---

## Thinning Process

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Remove weak heroes |
| Method | Send to die in dungeons |
| Resource Savings | Food, gear, training time |
| Frequency | Regular (every few days) |
