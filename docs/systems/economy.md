# Economy System

> Resources, crafting, and facility management.

---

## Resources

| Resource | Source | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | Stage clears, quests | Regular summoning, upgrades |
| **Gems** | Rare drops, real money | Advanced summoning, premium items |
| **Food** | Restaurant, foraging | Hero sustenance |
| **Materials** | Monster drops, crafting | Equipment, facility upgrades |

---

## Gem Economy

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Free Gems | Very rare, hard to obtain |
| Paid Gems | Real money (ANYTNG spends 65,000 won packages) |
| Usage | Advanced summoning, premium upgrades |
| Strategy | Save for critical moments |

### Crafting Rules
- Exclusive weapons stay with heroes
- Regular weapons can be taken away
- Higher rank = better stats
- Materials required from monster drops

## Resource Management Strategy

### Han's Approach
1. **Thin weak heroes** — save food and gear
2. **Prioritize combat team** — they clear stages
3. **Upgrade facilities gradually** — don't overspend
4. **Save gems** — advanced summoning is expensive
5. **Craft before boss floors** — gear matters

### Master's Approach (ANYTNG)
- Reckless spending on gems
- Prioritizes summoning over upgrades
- Doesn't optimize resource allocation
- Han internally criticizes these decisions

## Economic Constraints

| Constraint | Impact |
|---|---|
| Limited food | Heroes get hungry, morale drops |
| Limited gems | Can't always advanced summon |
| Limited materials | Crafting is slow |
| Limited slots | Support roles are competitive |
| Thinning pressure | Weak heroes are dead weight |
