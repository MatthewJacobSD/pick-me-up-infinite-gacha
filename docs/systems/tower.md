# Tower System

> The 100-floor tower structure and difficulty mechanics.

---

## Structure

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Total Floors | 100 |
| Current Progress | Floor 4 cleared |
| Boss Floors | Every 5 stages (5, 10, 15, etc.) |
| Difficulty Scaling | Exponential after boss floors |

---

## Floor Mechanics

### Regular Floors (1-4, 6-9, etc.)
- Standard monster encounters
- Goblins, slimes, basic enemies
- Designed for 1★-3★ heroes
- Thin resource consumption

### Boss Floors (5, 10, 15, etc.)
- **Major difficulty spike**
- Requires fully trained team
- Unique boss mechanics
- Higher star-rank heroes required
- Even Han isn't 100% sure he'll survive

---

## Difficulty Progression

```
Floor 1-4: Tutorial zone (easy)
Floor 5:   Boss spike (medium)
Floor 6-9: Gradual increase
Floor 10:  Major boss spike (hard)
Floor 15+: Endgame territory
```

---

## Party System

| Mechanic | Description |
|---|---|
| Party Size | Varies by floor |
| Role Assignment | Combat vs Support |
| Formation | Drag-and-drop positioning |
| Combat Desire | Heroes perform better when willing to fight |

---

## Resource Drain

- **Combat heroes**: High stat requirements, gear consumption
- **Support heroes**: Lower requirements, but limited spots
- **Thinning**: Weak heroes sacrificed to save resources for strong ones

---

## Strategic Considerations

1. **Don't rush to boss floors** — prepare team first
2. **Balance combat and support** — can't fight with only DPS
3. **Monitor skill progression** — some heroes plateau
4. **Craft gear before boss floors** — equipment matters
5. **Thin regularly** — weak heroes are dead weight
