## Personality Traits

- **Conscience under pressure** — the only Wolf who objected to the unexamined synthesis spree ("Avant, let's stop here for today"); shouted down, but it foreshadowed her defection
- **Honest about fear and inadequacy** — acknowledged out loud that nobody trusted her as leader ("Looking at your expressions… I guess you don't trust me.") instead of posturing
- **Resolve after dread** — objects when a plan looks unwinnable ("we still can't win"), then commits fully once the decision is made ("I got it. We'll do our best.")
- **Dry, deadpan humor** — "That's a bad joke." (Ch. 25); "I can't believe luck is being used with fairness in the same sentence.." (Ch. 20)
- **Observant veteran eye** — read Eolka as "an immature lady" at a glance: "I can tell after being a mercenary for so long."
- **Filial loyalty** — stayed with the Wolves only because of her father's request; quit the moment that constraint surfaced

## Combat Role

Dagger-type vanguard. Han rates her skilled even among mercenaries. As Party 2 leader she runs independent operations (Floor 9 village subjugation ~50 goblins, Ch. 25) and now holds the **north outer wall** of Floor 10 against ladder carts (Ch. 26-27) — the defense's hardest assignment, with fallback to the inner wall and sanctioned bait doctrine.

---

## Concept Knowledge

As a 3★ summon she innately knows master/summon/synthesis concepts without explanation — she explained synthesis unprompted in Ch. 15 ("To become stronger by offering others as sacrifices"). She remembers her old life (Halsia, mercenary work) but not how she was summoned; probing the memory causes head pain.

## Key Moments

1. **Ch. 15**: Probed by Han for intel — confirms rank-based knowledge gaps; explains synthesis concept unprompted; recalls her Halsia mercenary past but not her summoning
2. **Ch. 16**: Sole voice of conscience during the unexamined synthesis — "Avant, let's stop here for today" — shouted down by Jaken's group
3. **Ch. 17**: Reveals she stayed with the Wolves only due to her father's request; defects to Han mid-duel — her quit disbands the Pulverizing Wolves
4. **Ch. 18**: Levelled up consuming the six-slayer batch + gains **Trap Disarm**; awed watching Han and Jenna fight ("I can't believe someone so young is so strong…!"); begins dagger sparring vs Jenna
5. **Ch. 19**: Reluctant about leadership ("I think Jenna would be…") → named Blue-lot leader anyway; addresses the trust gap head-on, then resolves it: "Don't worry, we won't lose to you."
6. **Ch. 20**: Lot contest — both leaders caught cheating by Jenna's remix; loses Eolka (*regretful*); escorts Roderick into the test stage: "You'll be the main character of this battle after all."
7. **Ch. 25**: Delivers the Floor 9 report (~50 goblins subjugated; ground muddy "like it has rained so much" — the linked-quest clue); "That's a bad joke."; asks how the Floor 10 challenge will work
8. **Ch. 26**: Reads the mission decode first ("Defend?"); handed the north-wall holding job — "N-No way…" → objects twice on feasibility ("even if we buy time, we still can't win") → accepts: "I got it. We'll do our best."
9. **Ch. 27**: Receives Shurn mid-battle, sent back by Han with a conditional acknowledgment promise
