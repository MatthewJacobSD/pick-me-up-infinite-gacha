## Personality Traits

- **Reckless**: Sacrificed 4★ Shay to enhance 1★ Han without thinking
- **Impulsive**: Spends gems on summoning instead of upgrades
- **Busy**: Limited online time (probably an office worker)
- **Curious**: Does research, spends money on the game
- **Oblivious**: Doesn't know heroes are real humans; approved a lethal duel without knowing the duel system existed (Ch. 17)
- **Vengeful when provoked**: After losing all five Pulverizing Wolves, vented his fury by synthesizing six slacker heroes into Edis (Ch. 18)
