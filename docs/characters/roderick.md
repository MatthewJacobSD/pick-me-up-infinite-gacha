## Personality Traits

- **Strong sense of duty**: "strangely high sense of duty" — feels hypnotized into obligation ("I feel like I need to do something… It's like I've been hypnotized.")
- **Pragmatic**: asks the right question immediately ("Must I fight with enemies that I don't know about here?")
- **Perceptive**: grasps the situation far faster than Eolka and coaches her through it
- **Dry**: deadpan agreement with Eolka's horror — "…I agree."
- **Composed**: accepts Edis's lead with a simple "Hmm. Alright."
