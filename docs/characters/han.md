## Personality Traits

- **Calculative**: Always thinking 3 steps ahead, knows game mechanics inside out
- **Emotionally Guarded**: Doesn't show feelings, keeps distance from allies
- **Pragmatic**: Will sacrifice weak heroes without hesitation
- **Observant**: Notices details others miss (skills, stats, behaviors)
- **Protective**: Despite cold exterior, cares about his core team
- **Self-Deprecating**: Knows he's trapped, accepts his fate with dark humor
