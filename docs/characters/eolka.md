---

## Personality Traits

- **Immature / sheltered**: "an immature lady" (Edis); collapsed on arrival from pure embarrassment, not injury
- **Slow to grasp situations**: Han ranks her below Roderick ("You're slower at grasping situations than the old man beside you"); dragged in with zero context and resents it
- **Proud**: finds being judged offensive; "How could they treat people like this…"; claims "You're going to be so surprised that you'll fall over after seeing my fire magic"
- **Resistant but compliant**: protests loudly ("This is so unpleasant!", "I can't believe I need to fight unknown place for unknown reasons!") yet obeys when synthesis is invoked as consequence
- **Self-proclaimed title**: "Rivel's Witch" — claims monster-fighting experience ("Of course! They call me Rivel's Witch!" — Ch. 21)

## Casting Limitations (Ch. 21)

- **Stationary cast only**: 1-minute cast time for "La Gran Sedus" — cannot move while casting
- **No barrier while casting**: must choose between offense and defense; can't sustain both
- **No mobile casting**: Han assumed defensive versatility ("A defensive system is more versatile than an attack system") but she can't actually execute it
- **Mage-first enemy priority**: Goblin Raiders (Floor 8) immediately targeted her with crossbow bolts while she was immobile mid-cast

---

## Combat Role

- **Primary**: Ranged artillery (fire magic) — powerful but completely stationary
- **Synergy**: Han's stated reason for taking her over the guard — "fire magic works better with archers" (pairs with Jenna's bow)
- **Rarity value**: magicians only appear through Gacha; irreplaceable by growth
- **Caveat**: Han assessed her as needing to be "skilled as she is arrogant" — the Ch. 8 test showed the gap between her claims and reality

## Key Moments

1. **Ch. 20**: Summoned as a Rare 3★; collapses in embarrassment on arrival ("She's probably more embarrassed than in pain" — Han)
2. **Ch. 20**: Accidentally reveals fire magic during the leaders' lot scuffle; both leaders freeze
3. **Ch. 20**: Assigned to Party 1 via formal invitation after Jenna remixes the cheated lots; joins over Edis's regretful objection
4. **Ch. 20**: Watches Yon & Zenin synthesized into her — levels up immediately; Han's lesson lands: "If you lag behind, that's what will become of you"
5. **Ch. 21**: Interrogation concludes — max third-circle fire magic, no support magic ("that's magic that a 3-runa would learn"), no skill window → awakening seemingly absent; Han decides she fights with them regardless
6. **Ch. 21**: Self-proclaimed "Rivel's Witch" with monster-fighting experience — then immediately exposed as unable to mobile-cast on Floor 8; 1-minute stationary cast ("La Gran Sedus") under fire, bolts nail her — cliffhanger
7. **Ch. 22**: Han physically rescues her from the open field, carries her into a forested zone ("Don't tell me she can't even use mobile casting?!"); she complains her hands hurt from mana/firepower output — "A handful?! Did you see the amount of mana and fire power I used?!"
8. **Ch. 22**: Finally casts from forest cover ("Ignite!") after repositioning — still not confirmed if the spell lands
9. **Ch. 23**: Full spell sequence — "Ignite! Burn! Transcend!" — destroys 20+ Goblin Raiders in one go; **MVP of Floor 8**; Aaron and Eolka level up; collapses mid-victory boast from total mana depletion; Han analyses: "tank without armor" — firepower for defense/mobility trade-off
