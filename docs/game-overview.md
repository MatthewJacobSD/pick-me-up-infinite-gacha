## Core Features

- **AI Hero & NPC Integration** — Dynamic, adaptive behaviour instead of fixed scripts
- **Lobby Hub** — Resource management, hero upgrades, alliances, events, missions
- **Tower Battlefield** — Main combat zone with Guide Wars, faction conflicts, layered strategy
- **Node Function System** — Battlefield divided into nodes; control grants buffs, resources, tactical advantages
- **Base Building & Expansion** — Construct and upgrade facilities to strengthen factions
- **Resource Management** — Gathering, production, protection, raids, defence
- **Alliance System** — Cooperative play, shared progression, raids, defence
- **Technology Tree** — Research upgrades, unlock new abilities, improve efficiency
- **Dynamic Events** — Seasonal campaigns, invasions, timed challenges, special rewards
- **Hero Deployment** — AI-driven heroes adapt to situations, unique battle outcomes
- **Strategic Campaigns** — Multi-stage missions, faction wars, long-term progression
- **Multiplayer Infrastructure** — Matchmaking, guild wars, cooperative raids, competitive ranking
- **Economy Systems** — In-game currency, resource trading, progression balance
- **Level Design & Map Expansion** — Scalable maps, mission flow, difficulty scaling
- **Audio Integration** — Music, sound effects, voice systems
- **Visual Systems** — 2D/3D art, animation, environment, character design, VFX
- **UI/UX Systems** — Player interface, accessibility, polish
- **Community Features** — Chat, events, engagement tools, etc.
- **Additional Systems** — Future expansions, new modes, cross-platform optimizations, etc.
