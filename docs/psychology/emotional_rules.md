# Emotional Rules

> Comprehensive emotional states, triggers, and responses for all characters.
> Based on Chapters 1-14. Ensures human-like emotional continuity.

---

## Emotional Continuity Principle

Every character must show consistent emotional responses across chapters. Emotional states don't disappear without reason. If a character is scared in Ch.1, that fear should influence their behaviour in Ch.2 unless addressed.

### Emotional Patterns

**Fear Response Chain (Ch.2):**
1. Initial panic: "What is this? Huh?"
2. Disbelief: "It's not realistic at all?!"
3. Acceptance: "But I didn't need a long time to accept reality"
4. Determination: "Watch me survive until the end"

**Anger Triggers:**
- Being deceived (Ch.8: "This is the last time I'll overlook this")
- Reckless master decisions (Ch.2: "the master was stupid")
- Weak heroes dying uselessly (Ch.1: "Shit...")

**Compassion Limits:**
- Allows Aaron to help (Ch.8) but sets boundaries
- Doesn't help weak heroes personally (Ch.1: "Ignore them. They won't be of help")
- Internal conflict: knows they'll die, but doesn't emotionally invest

**Suppression Mechanism:**
- Uses game mechanics as emotional buffer
- Refers to death as "returning to the goddess' arms"
- Treats heroes as units, not people (mostly)
- Only shows concern for core team (Jenna, Aaron)

### Emotional Gaps (Potential Issues)

| Gap | Risk | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| No grief shown for dead heroes | May seem sociopathic | Show internal conflict occasionally |
| No homesickness for Earth | May seem detached | Add internal monologue about old life |
| No fear of Niflheimr directly | May seem overconfident | Show strategic worry, not panic |
| No joy in victories | May seem robotic | Add small moments of satisfaction |

## Emotional Continuity Rules

### Rule 1: Emotions Persist
If a character is scared in Ch.1, that fear should influence their behaviour in Ch.2 unless addressed.

### Rule 2: Emotional Triggers Are Consistent
If Han gets angry when deceived in Ch.8, he should get angry when deceived in future chapters.

### Rule 3: Emotional Growth Is Shown
Jenna's trust in Han should grow visibly across chapters (Ch.1: questioning → Ch.8: complete trust).

### Rule 4: Suppressed Emotions Surface
Han suppresses emotions, but they should surface in private moments (internal monologue).

### Rule 5: Emotional Gaps Are Filled
If a character shows no emotion where one is expected, add it (e.g., Aaron's frustration with skill plateau).

### Rule 6: Death Emotions Are Shown
Heroes facing death should show fear, acceptance, or defiance — not just silence.
