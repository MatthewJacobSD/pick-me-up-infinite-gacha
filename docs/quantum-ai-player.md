## What is Project Quantum AI?

Imagine characters in your favourite game who don't just follow scripts, but genuinely think, react and feel. That's the essence of Quantum AI. It's an advanced intelligence system that allows non-player characters (NPCs), companions, and even opponents to make decisions based on their unique personalities and the ever-changing game environment. They learn, adapt, and respond in ways that feel natural and surprising, creating a truly immersive experience.

## Every Character, a Unique Personality

With Quantum AI, no two characters are alike. We've developed a system where each character is imbued with its own distinct personality profile. This means:

- A cautious healer might prioritize protecting allies above all else
- A bold warrior might rush into the fray with aggressive tactics
- A clever rogue might strategically flank or seek cover

These aren't pre-programmed behaviours; they are the results of each character's individual 'mind' processing the game's events. This ensures that every interaction, every battle, and every moment spent with your characters feels authentic and unique.
